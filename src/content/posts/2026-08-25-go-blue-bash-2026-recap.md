## The Idea Tree

Hundreds of you stopped by and hung an idea on our tree. Hammocks. More study spaces. Cross-campus events. A snowball fight (yes, really). Free clothing swaps. Support for the Student Food Pantry. We read every note, and all of it goes to the executive board and the Senate committees.

![The Idea Tree covered in student notes](/media/posts/gbb2026_ideatree.jpg)

## We planted a tree

Small roots today, big shade later. With the Environmental Sustainability Committee we planted a tree on campus during Welcome Week.

![Student Government members planting a tree on campus](/media/posts/gbb2026_planting.jpg)

## Charity 5K 2026

You got the first look at our fall run with the Wolverine community. Registration and details are coming soon. Follow the [Charity 5K page](/initiatives/charity-5k) for updates.
