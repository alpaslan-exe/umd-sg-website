The President is the chief representative of the UM-Dearborn student body. The office chairs General Meetings, meets regularly with the Chancellor's cabinet and the Office of Student Life, reports to the University of Michigan Board of Regents, and leads campus-wide initiatives such as the President's Roundtable.
