Student Government and the UM-Dearborn Athletics Department are bringing a Charity 5K to campus this fall. Students, staff, faculty, alumni, friends, and family are all welcome to run or walk.

## What we know so far

- **When:** Fall 2026. Date to be announced.
- **Where:** Starting at the Fieldhouse. Route details are being finalized with the university.
- **Who:** Everyone in the Wolverine community. No experience needed.
- **Why:** Proceeds support a campus cause. Details drop with registration.

Registration, pricing, and the route map will be posted here and on Instagram as soon as they are approved. Questions? Email the Director of Athletics from the Executive Board page.
