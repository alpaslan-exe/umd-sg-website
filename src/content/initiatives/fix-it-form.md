Launched in Fall 2025, the Fix-It Form gives students a single place to report anything on campus that needs attention: a broken outlet in the library, a confusing policy, a missing resource. Student Government routes each submission to the responsible university office and posts status updates so you can see what happened.

Until the form link for this year is posted here, drop your issue in SG Hears You or email any executive board member.
