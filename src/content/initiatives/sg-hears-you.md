**SG Hears You** is Student Government's open forum for the whole campus. It lives in a public WhatsApp group that any UM-Dearborn student can join. No forms, no waiting for office hours.

## How it works

1. Scan the QR code on our flyers or tap the join link above.
2. Post any concern or idea, big or small. Parking, food, study space, a class policy, an event you wish existed.
3. SG reads every message. Concerns are routed to the right executive board member, committee, or university office.
4. We report back in the group and on Instagram, and you vote on the projects we take on next.

## Ground rules

- Be respectful. This is a shared campus space moderated by the executive board.
- Keep it about campus. Personal disputes and private complaints about individuals are better handled by emailing us directly or by reaching out to the Office of Student Life.
- Anonymous? Use the **Meet with the President** form or email any board member instead.

Open to every UM-Dearborn student.
