The President's Roundtable brings student organization leaders together with the Student Government executive board. Past roundtables surfaced actionable concerns ranging from campus catering to programming logistics, and led to shared calendars and joint events between organizations.

If you lead a registered student organization, register through the form above and the Director of Student Life will send the date, room, and agenda.
