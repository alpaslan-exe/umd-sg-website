Passed by the Senate in Fall 2025, the Voice in Every Classroom resolution empowers Student Government to visit classes across all four colleges. A short, five-minute visit covers what SG does, the free resources available to every student, and how to raise a concern or run for a seat.

Faculty and instructors: email the President to schedule a visit.
