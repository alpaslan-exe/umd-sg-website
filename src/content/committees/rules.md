---
name: Rules Committee
order: 1
summary: Reviews every resolution and amendment for compliance with the Statutes and university policy, and audits the Statutes themselves.
---
The Rules Committee is the Senate's constitutional check. Before a Legislative Resolution reaches the floor, Rules confirms it complies with the Statutes of the Student Government (UMDSGS) and the Policies of the University of Michigan-Dearborn. The committee also maintains and audits the Statutes.
